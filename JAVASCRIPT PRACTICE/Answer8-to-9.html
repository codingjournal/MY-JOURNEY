Solution to Question No 8 and 9

Q8. Explain Variable Naming Rules Industry Standard

Answer:
As per Industry Standard, Variable name should be in camelCase. Example

let studentAge;
let totalMarks;
let firstName;

Q9. What are Comments in Javascript?

Answer:
Comments are ignored by Javascript. Check script and out put in console tab of the browser.

                <script>
                    let age = 43;
                    let name = "Johny Singh";
                    console.log(age);
                    //console.log(name);
